# Project Design Document: In All Your Radiance
*A Keepsake Memoir for Ritika Raj • Created by Aditya Kaundal*
*Inspired by the HTWKR (How to Talk to White Kids about Racism) Digital Book Experience*

---

## 1. Project Overview
- **Title**: *In All Your Radiance*
- **Recipient**: Ritika Raj
- **Author**: Aditya Kaundal
- **Aesthetic**: Minimalist, cinematic deep black canvas (`#0c0a09`), luxury warm ivory paper pages (`#faf5ee`), rich ink black typography (`#141210`), and burnt orange accents & highlights (`#c85a28`), editorial serif typography (Canela / Cormorant Garamond style), authentic page flips.

---

## 2. Dedication Cover Screen (The Hardcover Pretext)
- **Hardcover Book Object**: Realistic 3D perspective hardcover book featuring [`book external cover.jpg`](images/book%20external%20cover.jpg) (Ritika in the golden poppy field) as the front cover with 3D spine, page edges, gold corner trims, and foil typography.
- **Interactive Action**: Clicking the book itself or the `[ Open book ✦ ]` button flips open the volume and starts the soundtrack.

  - `Header:` **A Memoir • 2019 to Present**
  - `Title:` **In All Your Radiance**
  - `Recipient:` **Ritika Raj**
  - *"May your path get clearer every time you take a step."*

## 5. Interaction Mechanics & Page Gestures
- **Button Controls**: HTWKR-style side arrows (`Previous` and `Next`).
- **Touch Swipe Gestures**: Natural horizontal swipes on mobile and tablet turn pages forward and backward.
- **Desktop Drag**: Click and horizontal drag turns pages like physical leaves.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Left/Right arrows, Space, PageUp/PageDown, and Escape to close.
- **3D Page Physics**: Direction-aware page turns with perspective, rotation, and realistic paper rustle sound effects.
